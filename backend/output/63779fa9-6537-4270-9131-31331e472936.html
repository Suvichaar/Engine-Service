A Brief History of Planet Earth

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago from dust and gas in the early solar system. Over time, its surface cooled, oceans appeared, and life began to evolve. From simple organisms, complex plants and animals emerged, shaping diverse ecosystems across the planet.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Human civilizations arose thousands of years ago, building remarkable structures like the pyramids of Egypt. These ancient wonders reflect our ingenuity and cultural heritage, marking milestones in Earth's history alongside natural changes and scientific progress.
