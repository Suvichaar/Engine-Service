The Ancient Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, built over 4,500 years ago, are monumental tombs constructed during Egypt's Old Kingdom. The Great Pyramid, built for Pharaoh Khufu, is the largest. These structures were created using limestone blocks, showcasing advanced engineering and serving as burial sites for kings.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The pyramids symbolized power and religious belief in the afterlife. They aligned with celestial bodies, reflecting ancient Egyptian astronomy. Today, they stand as a testament to Egypt's rich history, attracting millions of visitors and remaining one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
