The Timeless Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, built over 4,500 years ago in Egypt, are among the world's most iconic monuments. The largest, the Great Pyramid, was constructed for Pharaoh Khufu. These pyramids served as tombs for pharaohs and were designed to honor their power and ensure their journey to the afterlife.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Built using massive limestone blocks, the pyramids reflect advanced engineering skills of ancient Egyptians. They aligned with celestial bodies, symbolizing spiritual beliefs. Today, they remain a testament to human ingenuity and are a UNESCO World Heritage Site attracting millions of visitors.
