The Timeless Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza in Egypt were built over 4,500 years ago during the Old Kingdom. The Great Pyramid, constructed for Pharaoh Khufu, is the largest and most famous. These monumental structures served as tombs for pharaohs, reflecting their power and beliefs in the afterlife.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Built with millions of limestone blocks, the pyramids were aligned with astronomical precision. They symbolize ancient Egypt's architectural skill and cultural heritage, remaining one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World and attracting visitors from around the globe.
