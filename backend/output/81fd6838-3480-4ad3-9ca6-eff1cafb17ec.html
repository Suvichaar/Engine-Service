The Ancient Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, built over 4,500 years ago in Egypt, are among the most iconic monuments in human history. These structures were constructed during the Fourth Dynasty as tombs for powerful pharaohs, including Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure. They showcase the remarkable engineering skills of ancient Egyptians and remain a symbol of Egypt’s rich cultural heritage.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Great Pyramid of Khufu is the largest, originally standing at 146 meters. Built using millions of limestone blocks, it aligned precisely with the cardinal points. The pyramids served as elaborate burial sites, filled with treasures and sacred items, reflecting the Egyptians’ beliefs in the afterlife and their devotion to preserving royal legacies.
