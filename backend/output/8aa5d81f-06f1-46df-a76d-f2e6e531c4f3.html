The Timeless Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, built over 4,500 years ago, are among the most iconic monuments in human history. Located near Cairo, Egypt, they were constructed during the Fourth Dynasty as tombs for pharaohs Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure. These massive stone structures symbolized power, religious beliefs, and the ancient Egyptians’ architectural skill.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Great Pyramid of Khufu is the largest, originally standing 146 meters tall. Built with millions of limestone blocks, it aligned precisely with the cardinal points. The pyramids served as part of a complex including temples and smaller tombs, reflecting Egypt’s rich culture and the importance of the afterlife in ancient society.
