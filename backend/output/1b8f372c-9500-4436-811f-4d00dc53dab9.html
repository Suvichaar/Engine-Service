The Timeless Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, built over 4,500 years ago in Egypt, are among the most iconic monuments in the world. Constructed during the Fourth Dynasty, they served as tombs for pharaohs Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure. The Great Pyramid of Khufu is the largest, originally standing at 146 meters, showcasing the remarkable engineering of ancient Egypt.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The pyramids were built using massive limestone blocks, transported and assembled with precision. They aligned with celestial bodies, reflecting the Egyptians' advanced understanding of astronomy. Today, they remain a UNESCO World Heritage site, attracting millions of visitors and symbolizing Egypt's rich cultural heritage.
