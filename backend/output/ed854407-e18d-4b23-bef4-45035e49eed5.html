The Timeless Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, built over 4,500 years ago in Egypt, are among the most famous monuments in the world. They were constructed during Egypt's Old Kingdom as tombs for powerful pharaohs. The Great Pyramid, built for Pharaoh Khufu, is the largest and most remarkable, showing the incredible skill of ancient Egyptian engineers.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
These pyramids were part of a larger complex including temples and smaller tombs. They aligned with stars and followed precise measurements, reflecting Egypt's advanced knowledge of astronomy and mathematics. Today, they stand as symbols of ancient civilization and attract millions of visitors worldwide.
