The Ancient Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, located near Cairo, Egypt, were built over 4,500 years ago during Egypt's Old Kingdom. They served as monumental tombs for pharaohs, including Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure. The Great Pyramid of Khufu is the largest and most famous, showcasing remarkable engineering and precision.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Constructed using massive limestone and granite blocks, these pyramids were aligned with celestial bodies, reflecting ancient Egyptian beliefs in the afterlife. Today, they remain one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, attracting millions of visitors and symbolizing Egypt's rich history.
