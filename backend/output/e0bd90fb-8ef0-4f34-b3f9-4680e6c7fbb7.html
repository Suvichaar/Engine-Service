The Story of Planet Earth

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago from dust and gas in the solar system. Gravity pulled these particles together, creating a rocky planet. Early Earth was hot, covered with molten rock, and bombarded by asteroids, which helped shape its surface and bring water.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Over millions of years, Earth cooled, forming a crust, oceans, and atmosphere. Life began in the seas, evolving into complex organisms. Continents shifted, climates changed, and biodiversity grew, leading to the vibrant planet we know today.
