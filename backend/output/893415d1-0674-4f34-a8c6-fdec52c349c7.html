The Timeless History of the Pyramids of Giza

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Pyramids of Giza, located near Cairo, Egypt, were built over 4,500 years ago during the Old Kingdom. They served as monumental tombs for pharaohs, including Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure. The Great Pyramid, built for Khufu, is the largest and most famous, showcasing advanced engineering and precise alignment with the stars.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
Constructed using massive limestone blocks, the pyramids reflected the ancient Egyptians’ beliefs in the afterlife. They were part of larger complexes with temples, causeways, and smaller pyramids. Today, they remain one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, symbolizing Egypt’s rich history and architectural genius.
