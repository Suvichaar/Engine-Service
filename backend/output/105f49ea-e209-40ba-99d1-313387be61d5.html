📘 Notes Chapter
India's monuments reflect centuries of rich cultural heritage. From ancient rock-cut caves like Ajanta and Ellora, to medieval marvels like the Qutub Minar and Red Fort, these structures showcase diverse architectural styles influenced by dynasties such as the Mauryas, Mughals, and Cholas. Each monument tells a story of art, religion, and history.

©ABC Classes
📘 Notes Chapter
The Taj Mahal, built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan, symbolizes love and mastery in marble. Temples like Brihadeeswarar highlight Chola artistry, while forts like Mehrangarh display Rajput strength. These monuments preserve India's past and attract visitors worldwide, inspiring awe and pride.
